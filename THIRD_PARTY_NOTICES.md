# Third-party notices

The project is Apache-2.0; dependencies retain their own licenses. `package-lock.json` records resolved npm packages and license identifiers. Redis and PostgreSQL container images are separate upstream distributions; preserve their notices and review the exact image licenses when redistributing containers. No provider contract, live odds feed, payment integration license or third-party trademark right is supplied. Keep required copyright and license files with redistributed dependencies.

## Initial source dependency inventory

[THIRD_PARTY_INVENTORY.json](THIRD_PARTY_INVENTORY.json) records available version/license metadata from lockfiles and local validation. It may include optional and development packages; it is not legal clearance or a binary/container SBOM. Preserve upstream license files when redistributing dependencies. Unrecorded terms must be checked upstream before bundling the affected component.
