# Code of Conduct

Everyone is welcome regardless of background, identity, nationality, beliefs, disability or experience. Be respectful, patient and constructive; discuss ideas without attacking people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, retaliation and deliberate disruption are unacceptable.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project. Report concerns privately to [kokutbon@gmail.com](mailto:kokutbon@gmail.com). Include links and context and request an uninvolved reviewer when needed. Maintainers may remove harmful content, warn participants or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Decisions should be proportionate and communicated privately to the people involved. The organization has a single technical maintainer and has not established an independent escalation panel.

Keep reports confidential where practical and do not retaliate against good-faith reporters.

## Confidential reporting contact

Email [kokutbon@gmail.com](mailto:kokutbon@gmail.com) with subject `[xBET] Conduct report`. Please include relevant links and context and identify any conflict of interest. Request an uninvolved reviewer where possible. KOKUTBON currently has one technical maintainer; independent review is not guaranteed. Do not post sensitive conduct reports publicly or use security advisories for conduct complaints.
